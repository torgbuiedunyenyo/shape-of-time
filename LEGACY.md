# Legacy boundary

This repository is a conceptual reset, not a new version of `auto-biblio`.

## Retired implementation

The former Infinite Library lives at [`torgbuiedunyenyo/auto-biblio`](https://github.com/torgbuiedunyenyo/auto-biblio). Its exact historical anchors are:

- deployed runtime on 2026-07-18: `a07d2f2f61176c3737b3b52a4a29dd3160039c29`;
- last remote `main` before this repository split: `d82586161098700c00d7fb62a3ce78d7f86d81d4`;
- local recovery stopping point: `2482343437295688794d53de1e6b2e9cad94ddf9`.

That repository remains evidence about failure modes and prior mechanics. It is not an API, schema, migration, UI, evaluation, or prose-compatibility target.

## Earlier Shape of Time

[`torgbuiedunyenyo/infinite-book`](https://github.com/torgbuiedunyenyo/infinite-book) is the earlier book and source-history repository. This project retains its expanded corrected world and the useful ideas of pagewise preparation, exact phrase travel, immutable exposed pages, finite story scaffolding, and generation ahead of reading pace.

It does not inherit the earlier implementation, recursive momentum summaries, last-write-wins fact store, or post-ending continuation.

## Deliberately retained

- A dependable e-reader as the containing medium.
- Pagewise reading and predictive preparation.
- Semantic movement through phrases without CYOA plot choices.
- Exact return to the originating passage.
- A concrete authored Shape of Time story and verified world source.
- Immutable reader-visible content and complete generation provenance.

## Deliberately retired

- The narrative-debt ledger and possibility-set calculus.
- Semantic-segment/chapter publication machinery and successor editions.
- The multi-service Turbo monorepo, Redis/BullMQ worker topology, and old migrations.
- Four-model routing, generated voice conditioning, model tribunals, and the contaminated corpus.
- The old catalog/institutional ontology, EPUB-native live storage, and compatibility routes.
- Historical plans, status files, hooks, audits, and nested agent constitutions.

Consult old history to understand a decision, never to infer a current requirement.
