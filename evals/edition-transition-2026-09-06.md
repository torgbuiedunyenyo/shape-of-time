## Mechanism and separation

Implementation a1bef58 records checksums of the generative runtime, prompts, original artistic
inputs, dependency lock and Node pin, together with effective generation configuration. A fresh
reader edition stores that record before any generation; each new provider reservation also keeps
its actual mechanism and deployment revision. The record stays outside creative source context.
The process compares the recorded mechanism before continuing tools or reserving new inference.
A mismatch pauses work without deleting the edition. Client-only interface edits do not alter the
mechanism record. Broader server/dependency changes conservatively require investigation; a checksum
is not itself a judgment that the artistic process changed. Provider-side changes remain outside
this application's control and returned model evidence remains relevant.

Browser history is scoped to the edition identity and creation time. Visits, bookmarks, discoveries
and their selected source quotations do not carry from development into the reader edition.
Original browser records remain under their old keys. Database and private media namespaces also
separate the development and reader editions.

Thirty mechanical tests, typecheck, lint and build passed before release. The new tests verify a
real Postgres reservation records its mechanism, changed renewal settings cannot spend into a pinned
edition, and saved receipts remain retrievable. Real persistent Web Storage across three processes
verifies that a fresh edition sees none of another edition's visits/bookmarks/quotations while both
editions' own histories survive reload. These are mechanical claims, not literary judgments.
