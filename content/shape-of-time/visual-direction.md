# Visual direction

The selected shared medium is **inked reportage with transparent color**.

Its binding qualities are observational varied ink; transparent, restrained color; tactile paper,
wear, and repair; natural perspective; and concrete faces and hands. These qualities should let
people, rooms, streets, landscapes, tools, products, maps, and documents remain specific while the
library still feels like one illustrated object.

Varied hand-inked line fixes bodies, objects, and spatial relationships. Transparent watercolor
washes, sparse colored pencil, and visible paper carry atmosphere without hiding the drawing.

Each book derives its palette, light, density, materials,
clothing logic, graphic language, and compositional habits from its own setting and viewpoint. There
is no universal Bay Area palette and no era receives a moral color. Faces and material evidence stay
legible.

This preserves the selected medium, without carrying forward the comparison scene's people, place,
palette or composition. The agent can choose references, inspect actual images and develop their
relationship with the prose as described in SPEC.md. The old mandatory visual-profile fields,
per-image approval sequence and division of all narrative jobs between media are retired.

Source wording and the limited extraction are recorded in SOURCE.md.
