## Prompt heritage

The useful “Time Travel Tropes: What's False vs. What's True” section entered the earlier prompt in
`backend/src/prompts/templates.ts` at commit `51335b6`. The later experimental prompt at commit
`77e0c74` added three short GOOD/BAD demonstrations for concrete place, scene-level observation,
and ritual or specificity instead of summary.

Those materials are prompt evidence, not canon. The current temporal-rules file adapts the old
guardrail to remove ambiguous language while preserving its anti-trope function. The craft examples
file contains clearly labeled short adaptations of the three teaching patterns, remains separately
excluded from baseline, and may be used only in the controlled experiment described by the
specification and plan.

## Continuation and child-book heritage

The earlier implementation recorded its main failure plainly at commit `c0d72b3`: the root story was
effective through roughly page 80, then circled after the supplied arc ran out. Commits `c6a6d6f` and
`91b35eb` added arc-phase pressure and instructed a running-momentum summary to find a new tension
after resolution. Commit `7117150` then added an explicit open-ended post-arc regime; `77e0c74`
replaced its vague adventures with the more specific Undertow premise.

This repository retains the useful lesson, not that machinery or its added plot. A story needs finite
movements and, after an authored arc resolves, a genuinely new dramatic engine grounded in the state
the ending created. Undertow remains separate source material unless the owner later chooses it. The
new system does not restore page ranges, an endless seventh act, running summaries, or a fixed
post-arc premise.

Child independence also evolved in the old history. Commit `c4f3d25` gave a new book its parent only
as page-one founding context and asked for its own entry point. Commit `6a69e07` made the referenced
subject sovereign. Commits `daad177`, `81433c6`, and `15e1e46` separated generated child trajectories
from the predefined Jay and Tan root. The current design carries forward the founding-context and
independent-trajectory principles while rejecting title-as-identity, page-one destiny, generated
mode taxonomies, global fact extraction, and sibling contamination.

The old prompt also sometimes fixed Clef as a drink even though the corrected source leaves its form
open. That is negative evidence: Clef may be realized locally by a book, but no inherited prompt or
visual record may determine its global substance, packaging, or method of consumption.
