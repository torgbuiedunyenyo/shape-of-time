## Proposed direction [VISUAL-DIRECTION]

The proposed direction is **Oakland Offset**: observed, human-scale illustration made to look like
matte gouache and graphite on warm uncoated paper, reproduced with restrained offset-print texture.
Faces, rooms, hands, streets, products, and maps remain concrete and legible. The speculative world
appears through material facts that are slightly wrong for their surroundings, not through spectacle.

This direction follows the story's actual strangeness. Future commerce is already embedded in
ordinary Oakland shops, property, services, and infrastructure (`world.md:L89-L97`), and Jay
recognizes Tan from an accumulation of small discrepancies rather than one spectacular marker
(`world.md:L170-L176`). It also supports both intimate courtship plates and evidence-bearing maps,
visas, wrappers, receipts, and tourist documents.

This draft names a direction for owner review before any image is allowed to decide canon. B2 must
still compare real generated treatments and stress continuity. Oakland Offset becomes authority only
through explicit owner approval; a merely attractive first output is not approval.

### Medium and reproduction grammar

- Naturalistic gouache masses over visible graphite construction; edges may be precise where the
  narrative fact requires precision and open where atmosphere can breathe.
- Warm bone paper, soot black, bay blue, persimmon, muted leaf, and one restrained fluorescent
  violet accent. An era is never assigned a single moral color.
- Printed registration shifts may affect environmental layers, maps, light, or documents. A
  character's body and face remain one crisp identity. A person exists once while unoccupied times
  continue to evolve (`world.md:L17-L21`).
- Most scene plates use a human-scale middle distance or a close material study. Maps and artifacts
  may occupy the whole folio. The root book uses single illustrations, not panel grids, speech
  balloons, or cinematic storyboards.
- Atmospheric uncertainty is not ontological chaos. Time has stable properties, causal influence
  attenuates with distance, and navigation fails at the edges because maps and currents are
  unreliable (`world.md:L20-L27`, `world.md:L142-L154`).
- Canonical labels, screens, legends, and document fields are typeset deterministically over or
  beside the painted asset. Generated lettering is texture only and cannot carry a factual claim.

### Forms within the same direction

- The root movement favors observed scene plates and close object studies.
- The Clef aperture may lean toward packaging specimens, shop ledgers, and trade evidence.
- The maps-and-visas aperture may lean toward foldouts, route diagrams, permits, and annotated
  institutional documents.
- The earthquake-tourism aperture may lean toward souvenir contact sheets and controlled-viewing
  diagrams. `world.md` establishes transformed historical-event tourism generally
  (`world.md:L299-L308`) but does not establish a 1989 earthquake visit; all specific earthquake
  content must be separately reviewed as an authored extension.

### Temporal maps and transit

**Proposed invariant:** Temporal maps describe changing three-dimensional currents, not a line of dates or a
tree of alternatives. Accurate maps are proprietary sources of corporate power, while underground
maps are local and incomplete (`world.md:L52-L69`). The shared authored graphic grammar is:

- A tri-axial origin glyph labels **Primas**, **Phantas**, and **Mystas** in full on first use.
- Primas is a solid persimmon stroke with triangular markers; Phantas is a doubled bay-blue stroke
  with circular markers; Mystas is a violet broken stroke with diamond markers. Shape and line
  pattern carry meaning without color.
- Filled arrowheads mark Major direction and open arrowheads mark Minor direction.
- Currents are volumetric ribbons or streamlines with directional ticks, density for speed,
  crosshatching for turbulence, confidence envelopes, and a visible revision timestamp.
- High-confidence mapped space is crisp. Low-confidence routes thin and break. Obsolete routes
  remain as pale revision traces rather than vanishing.
- Corporate maps use exact alignment, dense live data, access marks, and a reserved company-glyph
  space. Underground maps use the same axes but show hand annotation, joins, missing layers, and
  local corrections.

The PRMTT is a compact body-adjacent prosthesis, not a vehicle or room. Its authored topology is a
padded shoulder-and-torso harness that unfolds three off-axis crescent rails around the user. The
rails carry mirrored vanes, matte ceramic thermal pads, a physical grip, and one narrow status
strip. Folded, the unit fits a rigid case approximately 45 by 30 by 15 centimetres. Tan's unit has
precise flush joints; repaired and underground units may expose fasteners and replaced surfaces
while keeping the same topology.

Travel keeps one body spatially near its starting position while controlled movement, light, and
heat accelerate travel on the temporal axes. A significant journey takes experienced duration and
requires calibration (`world.md:L33-L50`). Transit images show exertion, elapsed-time evidence,
map revision, thermal change, and secured belongings rather than a portal effect.

**Proposed variation:** Map extent, route, current velocity, confidence, annotations, ownership layer, and
revision state vary by scene. PRMTT casing, repair history, model finish, calibration readout, and
travel clothing may vary while the device topology and map grammar remain stable.

**Left open:** The PRMTT's power source, exact internal engineering, full mathematical projection,
company name and logo, absolute scale of temporal coordinates, and Tan's home date remain
unspecified. Edge stations and remote routes require separate reviewed anchors before depiction.

## Prose and image division

Prose owns action, dialogue, causality, thought, motivation, and basic orientation. Images own
place, material evidence, atmosphere, relationship, contradiction, spatial knowledge, or a fact
deliberately left unspoken. An image can foreshadow or contradict a character's understanding, but
it cannot be the sole carrier of an action or cause required to follow the story.

Every planned image must declare:

1. **Narrative job:** “The image tells the reader …” completed with one concrete proposition.
2. **New visual fact:** information not stated in adjacent prose.
3. **Prose withheld:** what the prose deliberately leaves for the image.
4. **Continuity roles:** the exact character, appearance epoch, location, object, medium, and
   neighboring references that govern the result.
5. **Must remain / must change:** invariants and intentional changes.
6. **Incidental noncanon:** details allowed to appear but kept incidental unless explicitly approved.
7. **Alt-text fact:** an accessible equivalent of the image's narrative contribution.

Reject an image if removing it loses nothing, or if its best description simply restates the nearby
sentence. Practical applications include:

- A shop establishing plate reveals the upgraded clinic opposite an unrepaired bus shelter,
  future-payment bridge, and future-oriented stock. It does not merely show Jay at work.
- The payment plate lets prose carry embarrassment, suspicion, and generosity while the image
  reveals the bridge hardware, Tan's garment discrepancies, the Clef package, and the distance
  between their bodies.
- A repeated shop view changes retained objects and interpersonal distance to show developing
  familiarity. It does not illustrate a generic date.
- A temporal-orientation folio lets dialogue carry motives while the map supplies actual spatial
  knowledge, route confidence, and corporate ownership.
- A decision-threshold plate places Tan's ability to leave—the case, map, or permit—beside Jay's
  continuing shift obligations. It does not use a symbolic forked road.
- A Clef aperture uses packaging, price layers, and later souvenir treatment to expose its trade
  loop rather than simply showing consumption.
- A visa folio exposes sponsorship and return conditions through the document while prose remains
  with Jay's experience of dependence.

Narrative information carried visually must appear in accurate alt text even when nearby prose does
not repeat it. Decorative paper texture and registration artifacts receive no alt text.

## Reference and continuity rules

Each approved reference has one or more explicit roles; order never implies a role:

- `character_identity`: approved neutral face and full-body anchors for Jay or Tan.
- `appearance_epoch`: reviewed clothing, grooming, fatigue, injury, and age state for a named span.
- `location_geometry`: approved plan and views for the shop or recurring Oakland location.
- `object_identity`: approved phone, Clef, map, visa, or PRMTT topology.
- `medium_palette`: the Oakland Offset paper, paint, graphite, ink, and reproduction grammar.
- `composition`: a layout or camera relationship to preserve without borrowing incidental content.
- `preceding_image`: the previous approved image when local visual flow matters.
- `causal_image`: an earlier image whose object, location, or visible fact causes the new scene.
- `left_neighbor` and `right_neighbor`: two approved surrounding plates used only for a repair.

Every request manifest lists its references in persisted order, assigns these roles, and states what
must remain and what must change. A normal scene uses the minimum governing identity, epoch,
location, object, and medium anchors plus the preceding or causal image when needed. A repair may
use both neighbors. More references are not automatically better.

Only owner-approved outputs may become anchors. A rejected, failed, or merely first-generated image
cannot be used as a continuity reference. Incidental jewelry, signage, bystanders, product labels,
weather, damage, or architecture remain nonbinding unless the owner explicitly promotes the detail
into the approved visual record.
