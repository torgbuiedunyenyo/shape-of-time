## Canon invariants

- **[TIME-01] SOURCE FACT — dimensional time.** Time has at least three known axes: Primas, Phantas,
  and Mystas. Each has Major and Minor directions. Space and time are inseparable; further temporal
  dimensions may exist. (world.md:L11-L12)
- **[TIME-02] SOURCE FACT — evolving times and one traveler.** Past and future times exist and keep
  changing without an observer. A traveler who leaves a moment is no longer there; travel does not
  populate history with additional instances of that person. (world.md:L17-L18)
- **[TIME-03] SOURCE FACT — mutual, attenuating causation.** Moments influence one another in every
  temporal direction. Effects weaken with distance, so nearby times change most and the system tends
  to heal rather than magnify a small intervention. (world.md:L20-L21)
- **[TIME-04] SOURCE FACT — bodily duration.** A clock's forward count is a convention. Movement in
  any temporal direction wears and ages the body. (world.md:L23-L24)
- **[TECH-01] SOURCE FACT — PRMTTs accelerate travel.** A Prosthesis for Rapid Movement Through Time
  uses patterned movement, light, and heat to accelerate a journey. It does not teleport. A long
  journey can take hours or days of experienced time. (world.md:L33-L50)
- **[TECH-02] SOURCE FACT — navigation needs living maps.** Temporal currents form a changing
  three-dimensional field. A PRMTT must be calibrated against current maps; an inaccurate map can
  produce a wrong arrival or leave a traveler unable to return. (world.md:L48-L58)
- **[EDGE-01] SOURCE FACT — mapped boundary, ordinary laws.** The edges are the limits of mapped time,
  most remote along Mystas. Their danger comes from fast, shifting, uncharted currents, not altered
  natural laws or a hostile agency. Deep regions remain unknown. (world.md:L138-L154)
- **[ECON-01] SOURCE FACT — unequal temporal economy.** Future wealth extracts resources, labor,
  culture, and tourist experience from past eras. This is a structural power relation, not a neutral
  exchange between equally mobile populations. (world.md:L73-L85)
- **[ECON-02] SOURCE FACT — the past is changed, not frozen.** Future demand, ownership, payment, and
  infrastructure have reshaped 2025 Oakland. That change is ordinary omnidirectional causation whose
  direction reflects unequal wealth and power. (world.md:L87-L97)
- **[ECON-03] SOURCE FACT — mobility is classed.** Past-to-future visas are rare, sponsored,
  time-limited, and aggressively enforced. Policy protects future labor and the inequality on which
  extraction and tourism depend. (world.md:L105-L121)
- **[COMPANY-01] SOURCE FACT — concentrated but incomplete power.** The leading PRMTT company controls
  the best devices, maps, and transit infrastructure and influences immigration policy. It pursues
  extraction and edge exploration, but neither its maps nor its executives control all time.
  (world.md:L60-L69; world.md:L150-L162)

| **PRMTT** | A Prosthesis for Rapid Movement Through Time: a manufactured temporal vehicle/prosthesis requiring calibration and subjective travel time. (world.md:L33-L50) |

## Political and social authority

- **SOURCE FACT:** Temporal tourism transforms what it claims to preserve. The Blitz has become a
  managed spectacle whose residents perform danger for future visitors; residents' attitudes range
  from resentment to dependence. (world.md:L99-L103)
- **SOURCE FACT:** The most visited eras are the most altered. Complaints about lost authenticity do
  not stop tourists from producing that loss. (world.md:L299-L308)
- **SOURCE FACT:** Preservation zones preserve an aesthetic by withholding development from their
  residents. They are not ethical refuges from temporal exploitation. (world.md:L310-L314)
- **SOURCE FACT:** Future-origin prejudice casts past people as backward, criminal, diseased,
  sexually suspect, and economically desperate. The same words from either side do not carry equal
  social force. (world.md:L344-L354)
- **SOURCE FACT:** An underground provides illegal documents, routes, shelter, work, and identity.
  Jay enters the future legally but later earns this network's trust while fleeing. (world.md:L119-L121;
  world.md:L333-L342)

## Character and relationship authority

### Jay [CHAR-JAY]

- **SOURCE FACT:** Jay is a young man in Oakland in 2025. He works at a liquor or convenience shop,
  sells clef, and depends economically on future customers. (world.md:L168-L170)
- **SOURCE FACT:** He knows the practical signs of a future-saturated city but initially cannot read
  temporal maps or calibrate a PRMTT. His skill is close observation. (world.md:L172-L176)
- **SOURCE FACT:** He recognizes Tan from accumulated mismatches and gives her clef from kindness,
  curiosity, and attraction. He understands the unequal power and stigma around them. (world.md:L174-L178)
- **SOURCE FACT:** In the future he is technologically dependent, socially suspected, and humiliated
  by the work of adaptation. During his flight he studies, practices, and becomes a competent traveler
  under pressure. (world.md:L224-L230; world.md:L244-L257)

### Tan [CHAR-TAN]

- **SOURCE FACT:** Tan is a young woman from an unnamed future, wealthy and privileged through her
  father's senior role at the leading PRMTT company. She reads maps and operates PRMTTs fluently.
  (world.md:L180-L184)
- **SOURCE FACT:** She first approaches Oakland as an "authentic" tourist destination. Neural payment
  technology leaves her unable to use Jay's physical device; class embarrassment makes her defensive,
  while his gift disrupts her transactional expectations. (world.md:L184-L186)
- **SOURCE FACT:** Her repeated visits become friendship and romance. She is curious and uneasy about
  her inherited system, but she is neither an activist nor free of its blind spots. (world.md:L188-L190)
- **SOURCE FACT:** Her care for Jay is real but unequal. She has never had to treat his reality as
  fully as her own because she can always leave. (world.md:L192)

### Tan's father, authorities, and company

- **SOURCE FACT:** Tan's father has real corporate power but does not control the company. He loves
  Tan, obtains Jay's visa and housing, and also keeps the couple close enough to monitor. (world.md:L194-L200)
- **SOURCE FACT:** Public investigators presume Jay's guilt through temporal-origin prejudice. The
  company maintains a separate, better-resourced security apparatus with cross-era reach.
  (world.md:L204-L210)
- **INTENTIONALLY UNSPECIFIED:** The source does not settle what Tan's father knows, why he cooperates
  with the accusation, or which possible objective drives the company's pursuit of Jay.
  (world.md:L200-L210)

### Relationship and ending [PLOT-END]

- **SOURCE FACT:** Jay and Tan proceed despite recognizing how their relationship is perceived and
  despite the real asymmetry between their eras. (world.md:L216-L220)
- **SOURCE FACT:** Tan later leaves voluntarily for the Mystas edges as an adventure. She is not
  abducted or silenced. Her failure is that she does not imagine what her unexplained absence will do
  to Jay. (world.md:L259-L267)
- **SOURCE FACT:** Jay forgives her for his own sake, not because her conduct was acceptable. They try
  briefly to continue, but Tan will not do the work required to see him fully and confront their
  unequal power. The relationship ends. (world.md:L269-L280)
- **SOURCE FACT:** Jay returns alone to a changed 2025 Oakland and to the shop. He survives with new
  knowledge; the extraction system continues and the edges remain unmapped. This is the true ending of
  the six-part narrative. (world.md:L281-L285)

## Forbidden contradictions

- **[FORBID-DARK]** The edge cannot acquire a sentient antagonist or supernatural agency. Its known
  danger is navigational uncertainty in fast, shifting currents. (world.md:L140-L154)
- **[FORBID-AMPLIFICATION]** A local causal effect cannot become stronger merely by propagating across
  temporal distance. Influence attenuates and the temporal system tends to heal. (world.md:L20-L21)
- **[FORBID-DUPLICATES]** Travel cannot create coexisting replicas of one traveler. Leaving a moment
  means the person is no longer there, while that time continues to evolve. (world.md:L17-L18)
- **[FORBID-MYSTIC-JAY]** Jay begins unable to read maps or calibrate a PRMTT. Observation, study, and
  practice—not a special faculty—produce his eventual competence. (world.md:L172-L176;
  world.md:L244-L257)
- **[FORBID-2150]** Tan's era cannot be an unchanging numbered endpoint. All times evolve, and this A0
  draft leaves her future relational and unnumbered. (world.md:L17-L18; world.md:L180-L184)
- Tan cannot be rewritten as kidnapped, murdered, or involuntarily consumed by an edge conspiracy.
  Her departure is voluntary and her moral failure depends on that agency. (world.md:L259-L267)
- The ending cannot become permanent reconciliation, corporate defeat, or Jay's transformation into a
  savior. He and Tan separate; he returns to the shop; the system persists. (world.md:L269-L285)
- The past cannot be treated as an untouched original chronology corrupted from outside. Every time is
  always changed by causes arriving from every temporal direction. (world.md:L17-L21; world.md:L87-L97)
