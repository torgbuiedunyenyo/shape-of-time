# Shape of Time — First Root Movement

The complete world and the complete Jay and Tan story remain in world.md. This brief describes only
the first finite movement of the root book. It does not summarize or replace the later story. The
root book continues after this movement in further finite movements, with the complete source story
guiding it until the six-part Jay and Tan arc has actually resolved.

The movement begins with Jay at work in the Oakland shop, already accustomed to serving people from
other times without sharing their wealth or freedom of movement. Tan enters looking almost at home
and slightly wrong in a dozen practical ways. When she tries to buy Clef, her unfamiliarity with
Jay's phone turns an ordinary payment into embarrassment and suspicion. Jay gives her the Clef
instead. The gesture comes from kindness, curiosity, and attraction rather than heroism.

Tan returns. Their meetings spread beyond the transaction and then beyond the shop. The movement
follows their courtship through particular encounters in Oakland, keeping both their pleasure in one
another and the unequal facts of their lives visible. Tan can leave for other times. Oakland is
Jay's home and one of Tan's destinations; he has never possessed her mobility, education, or
institutional access.

As their relationship becomes serious, Tan's invitation to see her world stops being flirtation and
becomes a choice Jay must actually make. Their conversation gives him—and the reader—an intelligible
first account of temporal movement because the decision requires it: other times are places that
continue changing, temporal travel follows mapped currents, and a journey takes duration and skill.
The explanation should arise from what Jay needs to understand, not from two people lecturing each
other about facts they both take for granted.

The movement ends when Jay decides to accept the invitation with a clear sense that affection does
not erase the dependency involved. Travel has not begun. The choice completes this movement without
pretending to complete the root story. The next movement begins from the consequence of that choice;
it does not restart the courtship, summarize the remaining arc, or turn this boundary into a
cliffhanger.
